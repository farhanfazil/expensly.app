Privacy Policy

Effective June 1, 2025

Expensly is built on a single privacy principle: your financial data belongs to you. This policy explains what we collect, what we don't, and why — in plain language.

§1 Data We Collect

Expensly is a privacy-first application. All data you enter — transactions, budgets, goals, salary — is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to our servers.

  • Financial data (transactions, budgets, goals) is stored locally on-device only
  • We do not collect, store, or transmit personal financial information on external servers
  • SMS reading is processed entirely on-device via iPhone Shortcuts — messages are never uploaded or shared

§2 Information You Provide

When you use Expensly, the following information stays entirely on your device:

  • Salary and monthly budget amounts
  • Transaction details entered manually or parsed from SMS
  • Savings goal names, targets, and progress
  • Spending category preferences and limits

§3 Analytics & Crash Reports

We may collect anonymized, non-identifiable crash reports and aggregate usage analytics to improve app performance and stability. This data contains no personal or financial information and cannot be linked back to you.

§4 Third-Party Services

If you choose to connect your bank account via the optional Lean Tech integration, your data is handled in accordance with Lean Tech's privacy policy. This integration is entirely optional, clearly disclosed, and requires your explicit consent before any data is accessed.

§5 Data Security

Your data is protected using iOS device encryption and the security mechanisms built into the iOS platform. Expensly supports Face ID and Touch ID authentication to prevent unauthorized access. We recommend keeping your device iOS version up to date.

§6 Children's Privacy

Expensly is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address below.

§7 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will provide notice through the App or via email to the address associated with your account.

§8 Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at:
