Privacy Policy

Expensly · Last updated 2026-06-12

Overview

Expensly is a personal finance app. When you sign in, your expenses, budgets, and profile sync to your account via Supabase. A local copy is also kept on your device for fast access and offline use.

Account & cloud sync

When you create an account, we store your email address and sync your app data (expenses, budgets, profile, settings, and preferences) to our database provider (Supabase) so you can back up and restore data. We do not sell this information.

Data we store on your device

The app stores expenses, budgets, income and lifestyle preferences, app settings, and optional receipt photos on your device using local storage. If you enable Face ID or passcode lock, access is protected by your device security.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not use your expense data for advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not read your bank SMS messages directly — optional Bank SMS shortcuts are configured by you in the Apple Shortcuts app on your device.

Account deletion

You can permanently delete your account in Settings → Delete account. This removes your sign-in credentials and cloud backup. Data on your device is cleared when you delete your account or sign out.

Subscriptions

Plus subscriptions are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. We receive subscription status from Apple to unlock Plus features. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Optional bank connection (coming soon)

When bank sync launches, connecting a bank will use a regulated open-banking partner. Only the data required to import transactions will be requested, and connection will be read-only. This feature is not active in the current App Store release.

Photos

If you attach a receipt photo to an expense, the image stays on your device unless you export or share it yourself.

Notifications

If you enable spending alerts, notifications are generated on your device based on your budgets and imported activity.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@expensly.app or use Contact support in the app.
